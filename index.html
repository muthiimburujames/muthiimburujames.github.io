research

Research Interests

01 · lead

Federated Learning

My MSc thesis examined how multiple institutions can train a shared model without centralising raw data — and where that promise breaks down in practice.

02

Privacy-Preserving AI

Differential privacy, secure aggregation, and the trade-off between model utility and the privacy guarantees people actually need — how much of you should training a model really cost.

03

AI for Human Wellbeing

Where AI can responsibly support mental health and wellbeing, and where the risk of over-relying on an automated system outweighs any benefit it offers.
